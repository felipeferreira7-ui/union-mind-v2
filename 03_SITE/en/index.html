— FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Union’s boutique approach mean?

It means direct access to your project lead, a team assembled for the scope and hands-on involvement from briefing to delivery. Specialists and suppliers work under clearly defined coordination.

How do you assemble the project team?

We consider the format, audience, complexity and venue. We identify the production lead and the specialists and suppliers required, with responsibilities recorded in the plan.

How much does a corporate events agency in São Paulo cost?

The investment depends on the format, attendance and production requirements. The initial assessment is free; a detailed estimate requires an agreed scope and the relevant supplier quotes.

Which trade shows does Union Mind support?

We plan booths and activations for trade shows such as APAS Show, Automec, Expo Revestir, Feicon and Hospitalar. We confirm the calendar, organizer requirements and production feasibility for each edition.

Do I need a complete brief?

No. Objectives, dates, venue, attendance and a budget range are useful, but we can start with what you know. In the first conversation, we agree the information needed and the proposal timeline.

Does Union Mind handle the event end-to-end?

We can coordinate creative work, planning and production, including event design, audiovisual services, logistics, catering and registration. We agree the scope with you and can integrate your existing suppliers.

Who will lead operations on my event day?

We identify the production lead during planning. You know who to contact for decisions and coordination during the event.
