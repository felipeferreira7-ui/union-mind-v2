For brands looking to meet their audience

Launches, demonstrations, sampling and relationship-building events. We work with your marketing team to define audience participation, materials and operations in line with your brand guidelines.

What we can coordinate

01

Concept and participation

Creative concept, experience flow and communication materials tied to the campaign objective.

02

Space and production

Scenography, materials, suppliers and setup conditions assessed for the location and activation format.

03

Team and follow-up

Team briefing, guest service and reporting defined in the scope. When collecting contacts, we align purpose, consent and data destination with the client.

Deliverables are defined in the proposal according to objectives, venue, timeline and budget.

What does meaningful participation look like?

A completed demonstration, a conversation with a salesperson or an interested contact? Defining this before creative work helps select the activity and necessary records. Reach, interaction and sales opportunities need different measures.

Before we start

Can Union work with an existing campaign?

Yes. We use your campaign and brand guidelines to develop the in-person activation and assess production.

How can we review activation results?

We define what to record according to your objective and available tools. Indicators and follow-up responsibilities are agreed in the scope.
