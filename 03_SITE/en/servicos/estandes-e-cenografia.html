For exhibitors and marketing teams

We design exhibition booths and event environments around the available area, exhibitor manual and business needs. The proposal considers use of space, brand expression and production conditions.

What we can coordinate

01

Design and use of space

Layout, visual language and design presentation to discuss circulation, display and guest service.

02

Materials and feasibility

Material and supplier options, with assessment of timelines, venue conditions and technical requirements before confirming delivery.

03

Installation and coordination

Planning for access, installation, technical resources and dismantling in line with the exhibitor manual and approved scope.

Deliverables are defined in the proposal according to objectives, venue, timeline and budget.

Does the space help your team sell?

We start with what needs to happen in the booth: demonstrations, meetings, display or guest service. This grounds the design in real use and prevents products, staff and visitors from competing for space.

Before we start

Which documents are needed to start?

Area plan, dimensions, exhibitor manual, exhibition name, dates and brand guidelines. We also need to understand the products and how your team will use the booth.

Do you design spaces outside exhibitions?

Yes. Scenography can be part of conventions, launches and activations. Each solution is assessed for the venue, programme and setup conditions.
