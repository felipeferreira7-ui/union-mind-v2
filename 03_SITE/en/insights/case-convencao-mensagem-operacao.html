When the stage message
needs to survive operations.

A corporate convention where content, environment, reception and agenda pace had to speak the same language.

The starting point

The central message could not remain inside presentations. The experience needed to begin on arrival and remain present through breaks, transitions and closing.

The decision

Content and production followed one decision sequence: what each moment had to communicate, how the audience would move and what had to be ready for rehearsal.

The lesson

When message and operations follow one plan, the audience finds coherence before, during and after the stage.
