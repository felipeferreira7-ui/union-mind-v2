Who leads your event? How to assess an agency’s team

Questions about leadership, responsibilities and involvement to help you choose your event team.

Start with responsibilities

A portfolio helps you understand an agency’s work. To choose your event team, also establish who will lead decisions, oversee production and be responsible on site. This structure varies between companies and should be clear in the proposal.

What to ask before hiring

Who will lead the project? How will that person participate in briefing, setup and the live event? Who coordinates event design, audiovisual services, logistics and catering? How does the team record decisions and changes? Ask for a simple responsibility matrix and confirm your contacts.

Experience relevant to your scope

Look for references with similar challenges: audience, format, infrastructure and timing. A larger event may need several technical leads. Assess how clearly they coordinate and whether the team is available for your project.
