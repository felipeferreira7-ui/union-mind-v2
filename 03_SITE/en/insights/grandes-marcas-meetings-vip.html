Trade show or executive meeting: which format fits your goal?

Criteria for choosing between a trade show presence and executive meetings based on audience and objectives.

Start with the audience and the conversation

A trade show and an executive meeting have different dynamics. Choose around whom you need to reach, the conversation you want and available resources. One format does not automatically replace the other: they may serve complementary purposes.

When to consider a trade show

Consider the visitor profile, the relevance of the edition and opportunities for demonstrations and relationships. Plan guest service, product presentations and follow-up. Confirm audience data and participation conditions with the organizer.

When to consider an executive meeting

An invited group can allow a more focused agenda and more time for discussion. Review participant interest, availability, venue, content and hospitality. Invitations and the agenda matter as much as production.

Agree how to assess the event

Agree in advance what to track: attendance by your target audience, meetings, participation and follow-up contacts, for example. Choose criteria that fit the objective. Union helps plan the format and production around that context.
