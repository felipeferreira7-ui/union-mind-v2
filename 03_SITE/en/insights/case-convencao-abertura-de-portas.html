The audience forms an opinion
before the first words.

A convention built around details that look small until they fail.

The real issue

Arrival, signage, registration, catering flow, cleaning and the agenda rhythm still had the power to define the audience perception.

The method

The team walked the participant journey, defined support points and assigned responsibility for situations that cannot wait for a crisis meeting.

The lesson

The audience experience begins when someone parks, looks for information or joins a queue.
